# Garmin Jr & Bounce Integration for Home Assistant

Custom Home Assistant component for Garmin Jr. and Garmin Bounce smartwatches. Provides GPS location tracking for map and zone-based presence detection, as well as step counts, battery levels, active minutes, and sync telemetry.

---

## Features

- **Device Tracker (`device_tracker.<child_name>_location`)**:
  - Real-time / periodic GPS coordinates and accuracy.
  - Automatic Home Assistant Zone mapping (`zone.home`, `zone.school`, etc.) for presence automations.
- **Sensors**:
  - `sensor.<child_name>_daily_steps`: Daily step count.
  - `sensor.<child_name>_battery`: Battery level percentage and status.
  - `sensor.<child_name>_active_minutes`: Daily active minutes.
  - `sensor.<child_name>_last_sync`: Timestamp of the last device synchronization.
- **Reliable Authentication**:
  - Supports direct Garmin account login with Two-Factor Authentication (MFA).
  - Supports importing pre-authenticated session tokens (`garmin_tokens.json`) to easily bypass Cloudflare bot challenges.

---

## Why Use This Instead of Watch Wi-Fi Presence?

Smartwatches (like the Garmin Bounce) put their Wi-Fi radio to sleep within minutes of inactivity to preserve battery life. Standard router- or ping-based Wi-Fi presence tracking falsely marks the watch as `away` whenever it enters power-saving mode.

This integration solves this by tracking the watch via Garmin's cloud/LTE telemetry, allowing reliable zone presence without depending on active Wi-Fi connections.

## Installation

### Option 1: HACS (Recommended)
1. In Home Assistant, open **HACS** > **Integrations**.
2. Click the three dots in the top right corner and select **Custom repositories**.
3. Add the repository URL: `https://github.com/ppelleti/ha-garmin-jr` with category **Integration**.
4. Click **Download**, then restart Home Assistant.

### Option 2: Manual Installation
1. Copy the `custom_components/garmin_jr` directory into your Home Assistant `<config>/custom_components/` directory.
2. Restart Home Assistant.

---

## Configuration

1. In Home Assistant, go to **Settings** > **Devices & Services** > **Add Integration**.
2. Search for **Garmin Jr**.
3. Choose your authentication method:
   - **Login with Email and Password**: Enter your Garmin credentials. If MFA is enabled on your account, you will be prompted for the code.
   - **Import Saved Session Token**: Select or paste your `garmin_tokens.json` generated by `garmin_token.py`.

## Presence Tracking & Automation Examples

### 1. Notify when child arrives at school
```yaml
alias: "Notify: Son Arrived at School"
trigger:
  - platform: zone
    entity_id: device_tracker.son_location
    zone: zone.school
    event: enter
action:
  - service: notify.notify
    data:
      title: "School Arrival"
      message: "Your son has arrived safely at school."
```

### 2. Low Battery Alert
```yaml
alias: "Notify: Garmin Watch Low Battery"
trigger:
  - platform: numeric_state
    entity_id: sensor.son_battery
    below: 20
action:
  - service: notify.notify
    data:
      title: "Watch Battery Low"
      message: "Garmin Bounce battery is at {{ states('sensor.son_battery') }}%. Please charge it tonight."
```
